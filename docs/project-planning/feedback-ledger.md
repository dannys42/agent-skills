# project-planning feedback ledger

`roadmap-plan-next-rung` was renamed `plan-milestone` in 1.0.0; older rows use the old name.

The standing brief for refining `system-design`, `risk-first-roadmap`, and `plan-milestone` (in `plugins/project-planning/`) from real-run feedback. The `/retro-feedback` skill (one command for both plugins' ledgers) reads this first and updates it after every round. Keep it short and factual; it is working state.

## Objective

Help the owner agree a project's structure first and grow it toward the goal on a foundation, accurately and at low token cost. Owner-led: the design starts from the owner's sketch when there is one, design sessions let the owner work a shape out before anything is recorded, and owner preferences carry their tastes into every first pass. Agreed: the system design holds subsystems, seams, the data model, build targets, names and a threat and privacy section for the current milestone only, stops at the additive check, and the owner reviews field by field only database schema, wire APIs and public symbols. Ordered: milestones are capability layers built foundation first in the owner's order, and risk orders rungs only inside the current milestone. Accurate: each rung folds itself back against the brief and escalates what it may not judge, and milestone close re-reads every fold-back and holds the owner's demo. Cost: Opus plans and closes a milestone rather than each rung, every line in a skill is read on every run, and every question costs the owner time.

## Rules for changing the skills

1. **Evidence tiers.** Tag every incoming claim `measured` (counted from docs, tasklists, or git), `observed` (seen in one session), or `guess`. Guesses never become rules; at most they become something to collect.
2. **One run is a hypothesis.** A change backed by one run ships only if it is cheap (a line or two), reversible, and has a named "Judge by" signal. Otherwise collect and wait for a second run.
3. **Every rule costs tokens in every run.** Prefer deleting or tightening a rule over adding one.
4. **Protect accuracy.** Do not thin the brief, skip fold-back, or drop a question category to save tokens without evidence that tasks did not have to fill the gap (no rise in `Found during` tasks, rework, or drift).
5. **Prefer collect over apply** when waiting one run is cheap. Claims that need evidence go under "Collect next run".
6. **No scripts or mandatory fields** without measured savings.
7. **Ship mechanics.** Bump the version in all four `plugins/project-planning` manifests (`.claude-plugin`, `.codex-plugin`, `.cursor-plugin`, `gemini-extension.json`) and in `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json`; add a `CHANGELOG.md` entry; record the change below with its "Judge by" signal.

## Signals available

Since 0.5.0 the rung retro also reads the task-workflow metrics log through `metrics summary` (run numbers against the median of earlier rungs) and `quality`/`quality_outcome` events from `code-quality-pass`. Otherwise evidence comes from the retrospective's feedback block (`retrospective.md` in `roadmap-plan-next-rung`): design-doc line counts before and after, questions asked and answers that changed the draft, assumptions overturned at fold-back, drift between brief and code, `Found during`/`INVALID`/`SUPERSEDED` tasks and raised `Model` tiers in the rung's tasklist, subsystem promotions, diagram render failures, missing-sibling notices. 1.0.0: the rung retro becomes one milestone retro (plus a short one for an unusual rung), and the `step` events gain token counts and `proposed`/`accepted` counts.
